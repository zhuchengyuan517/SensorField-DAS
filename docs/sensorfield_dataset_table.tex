Dataset name & SensorField-DAS & A field-oriented DAS benchmark for multimodal and multi-task sensor-field perception under cross-condition evaluation. \\

Released records & 13,806 & Includes 5,899 background-noise, 1,470 human-activity, 1,957 mechanical-excavation, and 4,480 vehicle-driving records. \\

Source coverage & 150 sensing zones & Each original source recording spans a distributed sensing field with 150 spatial monitoring zones before localized sample extraction. \\

Event taxonomy & 4 coarse-grained classes & \textit{background noise}, \textit{human activity}, \textit{mechanical excavation}, and \textit{vehicle driving}. \\

Fine-grained labels & 6 subclasses & Human activity includes \textit{walking}, \textit{striking}, and \textit{hoeing}; mechanical excavation includes \textit{construction}, \textit{excavation}, and \textit{cutting}. \\

Threat-location labels & 3 classes & Mechanical excavation records with valid distance are annotated as alarm $(0,5]$ m, tracking $(5,20]$ m, or no-threat $(20,40]$ m. Other event types are unlabeled for this task. \\

Soil conditions & 3 & Supports cross-soil evaluation under heterogeneous field environments. \\

Native sampling rates & 2/10 kHz & Source data are collected under 2 kHz and 10 kHz acquisition settings. \\

Observation window & 5 s & Each released record corresponds to a fixed five-second sensing window for benchmarking. \\

Standardized raw input & $1 \times 10000$ & Raw waveform inputs are standardized to 10,000 temporal samples for model benchmarking. \\

Spatial width $C$ & $\{1,\;6\}$ & Human-activity and vehicle records are single-channel, while mechanical-excavation records retain 6 adjacent channels. Each 10-channel vehicle source field yields 10 independent records. \\

Aligned modalities & 3 & Each sample provides raw waveform, space-time-frequency (STF), and Gramian angular field (GAF) representations. \\

Released sample form & $X \in \mathbb{R}^{T \times C}$ & Each record preserves a localized temporal-spatial field response rather than a full raw fence matrix. \\

Evaluation protocols & IID + 3 condition-disjoint protocols & The benchmark uses a source-group-disjoint 7:2:1 IID split and three condition-disjoint settings: region-level, soil-level, and acquisition-level. \\
